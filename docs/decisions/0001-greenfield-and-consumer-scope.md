# ADR 0001: greenfield consumer scope

Employee+ is built from zero in this repository. Prior personal projects may inform product lessons, but no source code, assets or implementation files are copied. The product is consumer-first because a purely internal B2B workflow is not an eligible Alexa+ direction. Providers, bookings, addresses and documents are synthetic. Payments, tax filing, identity verification, biometrics, video, audio and lock control are out of scope.
